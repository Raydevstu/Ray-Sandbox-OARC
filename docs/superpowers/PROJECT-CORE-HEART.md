Until every active slice is implemented and verified, runtime contracts, user experience, connectors, benchmarks, and acceptance gates must be checked against the active blueprint and current dated roadmap.

Required evidence discipline:

- `IMPLEMENTED` means code or contract exists.
- `TESTED` means focused automated evidence exists.
- `LOCALLY VERIFIED`, `BROWSER VERIFIED`, `BENCHMARK VERIFIED`, `LIVE CONNECTOR VERIFIED`, and `PRODUCTION VERIFIED` require their corresponding direct evidence.
- Missing evidence remains `PENDING` or `PARTIAL`.

Do not mark the platform, a connector, a quality claim, or a deployment complete from a build, typecheck, synthetic fixture, HTTP reachability, or local container alone.
